A more private first step towards men's health care.

MENOVA is a concept for helping men organise concerns, understand when professional assessment may be appropriate, and choose what information they share with a healthcare professional.

Prototype concept

01

Private intake

A structured way to describe concerns, relevant history and previous tests without starting with a public conversation.

02

Clear next steps

Supportive navigation towards self-care information, primary care or specialist assessment, with urgent warning signs prioritised.

03

User-controlled sharing

The intended workflow lets a person review and approve information before it is shared with a clinician.

How the proposed experience works

1 · DescribeShare a concern through a guided intake or personal diary.
2 · OrganiseRelevant details are organised into a readable health summary.
3 · ReviewThe person checks what is included and chooses what to share.
4 · ConnectUse the summary to support a conversation with an appropriate health professional.

Safety and privacy by design

MENOVA is a demonstration concept, not a medical device or clinical service. It does not diagnose infertility, calculate a validated risk score, interpret laboratory tests or replace a healthcare professional. Do not enter real personal or patient information.

Consent first

The product direction is explicit, user-controlled sharing rather than automatic disclosure.

Clinical boundaries

Screening support must remain distinct from diagnosis and treatment decisions.

Data minimisation

A production version would require secure identity, access controls, encryption, auditability and a clear retention policy.

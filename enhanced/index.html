Privacy and member control

Records are stored in the MENOVA Supabase project. Row-level security restricts each member's records to that authenticated account. This is a prototype, not a production-certified clinical record.

Production safeguards still required

  • Independent penetration testing and privacy impact assessment.
  • Verified clinician credential checks and privileged administrative workflow.
  • Audited, revocable, purpose-limited consent for clinician access.
  • Secure report-file storage, retention and deletion controls.
  • Clinical governance, safety evaluation and monitoring.
  • Configured leaked-password protection and account recovery.
